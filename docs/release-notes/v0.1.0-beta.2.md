## Gym Buddy v0.1.0-beta.2 — Android beta (test build)

Second Android beta. Pre-release for testers.

### What's new since beta.1
- **Camera permission:** the first time you take a meal or water photo, Android asks for camera access. If you refuse, the app explains how to allow it later; picking from the gallery still works without any permission.
- **Notification permission:** on Android 13 and newer, the app asks once on first launch whether it may send notifications (water reminders). If you skip it, you are asked again when you turn reminders on.

AI and backend are unchanged from beta.1. The AI features (planner, chat, meal and water analysis, calorie and body-fat advice) run through the Supabase `ai-proxy` function.

### Install / update
1. **If beta.1 is installed, uninstall it first.** Both betas are signed with a temporary CI debug key that differs between builds, so Android refuses to update in place. Data that was synced to your account is kept; just log in again.
2. Download `GymBuddy-0.1.0-beta.2-debug-signed.apk` below, open it, and allow "install unknown apps" if asked. Requires Android 7.0 or newer.
3. Optional: check the file against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

### Permissions requested
| Permission | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Notifications | first launch / turning on reminders | water reminders |
| Camera | first "Take photo" | meal and water photo analysis |

No location, contacts, microphone or storage permissions.

### Testing status
- Automated: typecheck, 56 unit tests, bundle secret scan, APK checks in CI.
- The new permission dialogs were **not yet tested on a device or emulator**. Everything else matches beta.1, which was tested on an Android 16 emulator (see `docs/BETA_RELEASE_REPORT.md`).

### Known issues
- Exercise illustrations are not generated (no image quota on the Gemini key); a placeholder is shown.
- E-mail confirmation and password-reset links open the app only if the redirect URL is set in Supabase.
- No Google sign-in yet. Reminders may arrive a few minutes late (inexact alarms).
- The free Supabase plan pauses after about 7 days without use.
- The UI is Turkish only.
